Background
In collaboration with the American Heart Association, we were challenged to answer: "How might we ensure AI and other emerging technologies close—not widen—the gaps in health outcomes and access?"
Focusing on systemic inefficiencies in the U.S. healthcare system, I conducted in-depth interviews with local physicians to uncover the friction points in their daily workflow and patient interactions.
However, the validation process revealed significant barriers.
Users expressed deep mistrust regarding AI in clinical settings and raised valid concerns about data privacy and security.
Instead of navigating the red tape of the US healthcare system, I shifted my focus to preventative care.
